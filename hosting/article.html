Gas Turbine

Through power-sector investing and behind-the-meter research, I had long known about GE Vernova and gas turbines as a choke point. But I have to confess: I underestimated them. I thought they were easy to make and not particularly big. Seeing one, I realized how complex, interesting and beautiful they are. So I made this interactive model to atone for my ignorance, HH.做电力投资和 behind-the-meter 研究时, 我早就知道 GE Vernova 和燃气轮机是一个 choke point. 但我得承认, 我低估了它们, 以为没多难做, 也没多大. 真正看到才发现, 它们比想象中复杂和有趣, 而且非常漂亮. 所以做了这个交互模型, 算是为自己无知的赎罪, HH.

Explore the gas turbine ↗打开交互模型 ↗GitHub · MIT ↗

The model is composable, with parts you can separate and inspect. I also made a cooled-blade study. Real turbine blades use cooling and coatings to work in gas hotter than the alloy's melting point while keeping the metal cooler. That cooling design in the real blade is what I find beautiful.模型是 composable 的, 可以拆开并放大看部分组件. 我还单独做了冷却叶片的原理模型. 真实叶片靠冷却和涂层, 能在温度超过合金熔点的燃气中工作, 同时让金属保持更低的温度. 我觉得真实叶片的这种冷却设计非常漂亮.
