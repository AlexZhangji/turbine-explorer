A gas turbine you can take apart把燃气轮机拆开来看

A machine becomes more interesting when you can see how its parts fit together, then pull one out and look closer.一台机器最有意思的地方，不只是外观，而是看清部件如何组成整体，再把其中一个取出来，凑近看。

Explore the gas turbine ↗打开交互模型 ↗GitHub · MIT ↗

That is what I wanted to build here: not just a turbine-shaped object, but a machine you can take apart. The casing opens, the assemblies separate, and selected parts can be inspected on their own. The model is written in Three.js, with geometry generated in code.我想做的不是一个长得像燃气轮机的物体，而是一台可以拆开看的机器：打开机壳，让组件展开，再进入少数值得仔细看的部件。模型用 Three.js 实现，几何结构由代码生成。

Start with the whole machine先看整体，再看零件

  1. Begin with the cutaway. Drag to orbit and scroll to move closer.从剖面开始，拖动旋转，滚轮拉近，先看清整机的内部布局。
  2. Switch to the exploded view. Look at how the casing, shaft and blade rows belong to the same assembly.切到爆炸图，看机壳、主轴和叶片级如何组成同一台机器。
  3. Start the airflow and rotation illustration. Motion makes the distinction between rotating and stationary parts easier to see. Sound is optional.开启气流和转动示意，观察哪些零件在转、哪些固定不动。声音可以单独开关。
  4. Enter the cooled-blade study. Cooling passages and coating layers are visible from the start.进入冷却叶片检视。内部冷却流道和涂层分解默认展开，不用先翻一堆菜单。

The compact Quality panel controls rendering and background across the machine and close-ups. Try the white blueprint or studio backgrounds, or raise the quality for a recording.小型画质面板可以统一调整整机和特写的渲染画质与背景。可以试试白底蓝线和摄影棚背景，录屏时也可以临时提高画质。

The blade is the close-up值得拉近看的，是那枚叶片

The interesting part is how materials and cooling work together. A representative thermal-barrier system combines a nickel-based superalloy substrate, a metallic bond coat, a protective oxide and a ceramic top coat. Internal air cooling and surface film cooling help control metal temperature. This is not a water pipe inside the blade, and hot gas temperature is not the same as the temperature of the metal.它有意思的地方，在于材料与冷却如何共同工作。一套典型的热障体系包括镍基高温合金基体、金属粘结层、保护性氧化层和陶瓷面层，再结合内部空气冷却与表面气膜冷却，控制金属温度。不是在叶片里接一根水管；燃气温度也不等于叶片金属本身的温度。

The close-up illustrates those principles. It does not claim to reproduce GE's undisclosed alloy recipe or passage layout. Layer spacing is exaggerated so you can see the stack; the neutral finishes are illustrative rather than measured material colors.这个特写展示的是这些原理，不冒充 GE 未公开的合金配方或内部流道。涂层间距被放大，是为了看清分层；中性的表面质感也是视觉示意，不是实物颜色测量。
