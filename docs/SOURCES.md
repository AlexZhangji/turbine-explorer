# Technical sources and limits

This project interprets public material; it is not a dimensionally validated engineering model.

- GE Vernova 9HA product information: https://www.gevernova.com/gas-power/products/gas-turbines/9ha
- HA architecture: https://www.gevernova.com/gas-power/resources/articles/2025/the-evolution-of-the-ha-gas-turbines
- Heavy-duty operating and maintenance considerations: https://www.gevernova.com/content/dam/gepower-new/global/en_US/downloads/gas-new-site/resources/reference/ger-3620p-heavy-duty-gas-turbine-operating-and-maintenance-considerations.pdf
- DOE / NETL coatings chapter: https://netl.doe.gov/sites/default/files/gas-turbine-handbook/4-4-2.pdf
- Nickel superalloy background: https://smg.msm.cam.ac.uk/outreach/articles/nickel-superalloy-turbine-blade
- Turbine cooling background: https://www.nasa.gov/special-projects-laboratory-turbine-cooling/

The blade illustrates a representative nickel-based superalloy, MCrAlY bond coat,
alumina thermally grown oxide and YSZ thermal barrier. The actual 9HA.02 alloy grade,
crystal structure, coating recipe and internal passage geometry were not confirmed.
Coating thickness and separation are exaggerated. Grey finishes are illustrative, not measured color data.

Blade counts, profiles, clearances, casing thickness and hidden piping are approximations.
The 16-can combustor arrangement is an inference, not a confirmed current product specification.
Exploded positions describe component relationships, not a validated lifting sequence.
Rotation and airflow are slowed or stylized for legibility; they are not fluid or thermal simulations.

The energy display uses the cited nominal 9HA.02 output and a selectable daily household demand.
It compares energy quantities, not guaranteed household supply, and excludes transmission losses.

Reference images, PDFs and video copies remain outside the public distribution.
